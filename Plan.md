#### One Sentence Pitch

- An accessible wellbeing companion app for dialysis patients that reduces travel stress and turns long monotonous sessions into a guided, meaningful routine aligned to the 5 ways to Wellbeing

#### Idea and Motivation for Project

- Dialysis sessions are frequent, tiring and time consuming
- For older adults like Zofia stress comes from unreliable travel planning, fatigue (from treatement), and boredom during sessions.
- Dialysis Companion supports a simple dialysis day routine that helps users prepare, travel confidently, stay engaged during treatementm and recover afterwards with minimal typing large controls and clear prompts.
